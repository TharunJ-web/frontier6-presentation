01

Core loop and the six budgets

Players build a mission, fly it and read why it worked or failed. Every part they add pulls on six coupled budgets, so one change ripples through all the others.

Player flow · 9 screens

A one-screen-at-a-time wizard with Back and Next. A live telemetry sidebar grades the design A to F on every click and names the most important fix.

04

Flight simulation and scoring

A launch roll, a cruise sized by distance, then a monthly operations loop. Twice per flight the game pauses for a tactical choice, then re-runs with the same random seed so only that decision changes the outcome.

Timeline
LaunchSuccess roll vs rocket reliability
CruiseMoon 3 months · Mars 8 months · further = longer
OperationsCollect science, generate data, downlink; storage caps the backlog
EventsSolar flares, micrometeorites, outages, leaks, glitches, bonus science
ResultsScore, objectives, anomalies, plain-language feedback
Score · 0 to 100 · 90+ shows SUCCESS
40%Science return
25%Objectives met
15%Efficiency
10%Reliability
10%Innovation

Progression

  • XP per flight = 25 + score + 20 per objective + medal bonus (a failed launch still earns 10)
  • Ranks: Cadet → Pilot → Navigator → Commander → Admiral → Legend
  • Medals: gold (all objectives + 85+), silver (all objectives), bronze (some)
  • XP shop: premium parts unlock with XP; spending never lowers rank

Example feedback

“Your instruments generate far more data than your comms can downlink.”

The report turns every weak score component into a reason the player can act on, then sends them back to redesign.
